This is CRITICAL.

Before publishing any quantitative claim,
classify it:

VERIFIED
→ Can publish.

UNVERIFIED
→ Remove, qualify or request evidence.

ESTIMATE
→ Clearly label as estimate.

ILLUSTRATIVE
→ Clearly label as illustrative.

NEVER invent proof.

Audit all existing claims such as:

- client numbers
- revenue influenced
- ROI
- sales growth
- response time
- retention
- industry statistics
- guarantees
- media recognition
- certifications
- security standards

If evidence cannot be established,
do not use the claim as fact.
